Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 16, 2026

HOMY ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how it is handled in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Who is responsible for your data?

HOMY
Contact: team@homyforme.com

2. What data we collect

When you request product updates or submit an idea, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your message or idea (if submitted via the "Share an Idea" form)
  • Date and time of submission

3. Why we collect it (legal basis)

We collect your data based on your explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you give by ticking the checkbox before submitting the form. You may withdraw your consent at any time by emailing team@homyforme.com.

4. What we use it for

  • Sending product updates you have consented to receive
  • Improving the app based on ideas submitted

We will never sell your data or share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Where your data is stored

Submissions are stored in a Google Spreadsheet, operated by Google LLC, under a data processing agreement. Data is stored on EU-region servers where possible.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data until you unsubscribe, withdraw consent, or request deletion.

7. Your rights under GDPR

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at team@homyforme.com.

8. Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies. We use a third-party IP geolocation service (ipapi.co) solely to detect your country and display the site in the appropriate language. No personal data is stored from this request.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product develops. The latest version will always be available at this URL.
